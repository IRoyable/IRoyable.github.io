How to play

Pick your device. It takes about a minute.

iPhone

Through Apple's TestFlight app.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.Get TestFlight
  2. Open the invite and tap Accept, then Install.Join the iPhone beta

Android

Through Google Play. Use the Google account that is on your phone.

  1. Join the tester group.Join the group
  2. Tap Become a tester.Become a tester
  3. Install Obstacle Chaos from Google Play.Open Google PlayNot showing up yet? Wait a few minutes after step 2.

PC (Windows)

Play with a controller or keyboard.

  1. Download the game from itch.io.Download for PC
  2. Unzip the folder and start Obstacle Chaos.Windows may show a "protected your PC" warning: click More info → Run anyway.
