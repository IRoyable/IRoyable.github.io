Obstacle Chaos - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Obstacle Chaos ("the game") is made by Roy Verlaan. This policy explains what the game stores and why. In short: no ads, no tracking, no selling of data. The only thing that leaves your device is what the online leaderboard needs.

What is stored on your device

Your settings, your gamertag, your personal bests and your local leaderboard are saved on your own device. They are removed when you uninstall the game.

What is sent to the online leaderboard

When you finish a course while online, the game sends this to the leaderboard:

DataWhy
Your gamertag (a nickname you choose, 3-14 letters/numbers)Shown next to your time on the leaderboard
A random player IDKeeps your times together and apart from other players with the same gamertag. On Android it is derived from a one-way scrambled version of the device's app identifier, so reinstalling the game on the same phone brings your profile back. It cannot be turned back into the device identifier and is not used for anything else.
Your race result: course, time, medal, chosen contestant and input type (touch, controller or keyboard)The leaderboard itself

We do not collect your real name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Please do not use your real name as a gamertag.

Where it is stored

Leaderboard data is stored with our database provider Supabase (supabase.com), which processes it on our behalf. Like any web service, the provider may briefly log technical data such as IP addresses for security. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

How long

Leaderboard entries are kept while the game's leaderboards exist. Leaderboards may be reset (for example at launch or for a new season).

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without an internet connection; then nothing is sent.
  • You can ask us to delete your leaderboard entries. Send your gamertag (and, if you can, your profile code from the gamertag screen) to IRoyableGames@outlook.com.
  • If you live in the EU/EEA you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Gamertags are nicknames; offensive names may be removed.

Purchases

If optional in-app purchases are offered, they are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: IRoyableGames@outlook.com.
